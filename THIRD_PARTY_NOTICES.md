# Historical questionnaire sources

The historical short questions remain their respective authors’ work. okaycupid is independent of OkCupid. The app's MIT license does not claim ownership of historical quotations or grant rights on behalf of their authors.

The 2011 question and choice quotations are attributed per question to the archived official OkTrends article/screenshot or contemporaneous Infochimps public listing. Dates, scope, and differences are documented in [the research notes](docs/matching.md#recovered-questions-and-evidence-dates).
